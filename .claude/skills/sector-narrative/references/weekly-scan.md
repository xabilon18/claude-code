# Weekly scan template

One short doc per week, titled `Weekly Scan — YYYY-Www (MM-DD → MM-DD)`. It has one section per sector (Energy, Semiconductors, AI Infrastructure) and runs to about 300–600 words in total.

The weekly's main job is to keep the ledger current. The doc the user reads only says what changed and why it matters.

```
# Weekly Scan — YYYY-Www

## Energy
1–2 short paragraphs: what moved this week, what it means for the story, and who it helps or hurts (named).
"Nothing that changes the story" is a valid entry.

## Semiconductors
(same)

## AI Infrastructure
(same)

## Watch next week
2–4 dated items, each with the sector it matters for.
```

## Rules

- The same rules as the monthly report apply: prose, no macro section, no tags or story IDs, named companies with reasons.
- A policy announcement is included only if it changes a sector's economics. Say how in one line.
- Skip a sector's section if nothing material happened there.
- All facts, tags, signposts and new candidate stories still go into the ledger update as usual.
