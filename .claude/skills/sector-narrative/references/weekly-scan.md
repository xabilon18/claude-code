# Weekly scan template

Title: `Weekly Scan — YYYY-Www (MM-DD → MM-DD)`. Length: about 600–1,200 words. The point is to keep the ledger current and catch new stories early, not to re-narrate.

```
# Weekly Scan — YYYY-Www (MM-DD → MM-DD)
**Ledger:** vN → vN+1 · **Last monthly:** <title>

## Headline
One or two sentences: the most important change this week, and which story it moves.

## Developments by story
For each story that moved (skip the rest):
- **<ID> <short title>** — what happened [V, source, date] → so what (one line) → companies affected (ticker, direction, mechanism).

## Political & policy announcements
Table: Date | Announcement | Actor | Stories | Why it matters.
List every relevant one, even if small: EOs, proclamations, BIS/OFAC actions, tariff notices, Fed speakers/decisions, FERC/NRC, state laws, foreign-government actions.

## Signposts hit
Bullets: story ID, signpost text, Confirm/Break, evidence.

## Predictions
Any that resolved this week (graded), and any now trending toward Hit or Miss.

## New candidates
Possible new big stories, each with the reason and what would promote it.

## Next week
Dated catalysts.
```

## Weekly rules

- Do not rewrite §1 Narrative thread of the ledger. Only append to its questions list.
- A story that escalates sharply (importance 5, or a status change to Escalating) gets one line flagged at the top of the Headline.
- If nothing material happened for a story, say nothing about it here. Monthly covers every story.
